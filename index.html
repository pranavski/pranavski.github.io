Amble

Shipped · App Store

An AI hangout planner for friend groups. Describe the plan — “brunch with four friends,” “game night for the squad” — and Amble suggests venues that account for group size, everyone’s preferences, the weather, and what’s actually nearby. Now on version 2.1.

Rule The privacy policy, terms, and moderation guide were written before launch, not after.

Soma

In development

A food–body record for people who cook. Ten-second meal logging by voice or text, read-only HealthKit, and a nightly insight engine that surfaces correlations worth watching. No goals, no calorie targets, no streak shaming — understanding, not dieting.

Rule Calories are always ranges — ~550–700, never a bare number. Insights stay hedged and gated on data coverage: worth watching, not a verdict. Never medical advice.

recs.fm

Open source

Music recommendations that actually listen. It pulls your Spotify history, reasons about it with Claude, and keeps the results so you can see how your taste moves. Neo-brutalist UI.

Detail Structured model output validated with Zod, prompt caching on the stable system prompt, and HttpOnly signed-cookie sessions that refresh Spotify tokens on their own.

  • Next.js 15
  • TypeScript
  • Spotify Web API
